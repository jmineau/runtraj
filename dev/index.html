runtraj#

runtraj runs particle dispersion models at scale. Give it the times and places particles are released from, some meteorology, and the settings to run under, and it runs the transport model once for each, on your computer or as a Slurm job array, and keeps the particles where you can find them again.

You choose the transport model and install it yourself. runtraj supports two:

  • HYSPLIT (HYSPLIT Integration)

  • FLEXPART 11 (FLEXPART Integration)

Another model, and what is made from the particles after a run (a stage), plug in by the import path of a class. PYSTILT’s footprint (stilt.footprint.FootprintStage) is one such stage.

The particles follow the air, backward from a measurement or forward from a source, over hours to days, at the resolution of the meteorology you give it. They carry no mass and do not settle. A transform weights each particle’s rows for a species lost on the way (to a lifetime, deposition, or oxidation) or for a column measurement. HYSPLIT’s other outputs are a mode of its block: concentration grids, and single trajectories.

Note

runtraj is alpha software. Names and options may still change between releases.
